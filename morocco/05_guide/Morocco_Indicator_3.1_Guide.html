GBF Headline Indicator 3.1 · National worked example with real data

Morocco, step by step through the 30×30 tool

This guide fills the Indicator 3.1 Excel tool for Morocco from the 429 records in the October 2026 World Database on Protected Areas and World Database on OECMs, the 67 official Key Biodiversity Area polygons and points (September 2026), and a GIS run over land and sea. Every number comes from those data and from the workbook recalculated in Excel.

Morocco shows two things the tool has to handle carefully. Most of its covered land is in 338 OECMs, chiefly permanent hunting reserves under shared governance, while protected areas alone cover about 2%. And the denominator depends on how Western Sahara is treated.

STEP 01

Collect the data

Everything used here is in the morocco/01_data folder:

  1. Protected areas and OECMs. WDPA and WD-OECM, October 2026, from the UNEP-WCMC map service behind Protected Planet: 91 protected-area polygons (42 of them still Proposed) and 338 OECM polygons, as shapefile, GeoJSON and GeoPackage. There are no point sites.
  2. Base map. Land: the geoBoundaries ADM0 outline, cut at 27°40′N (see Step 2). Sea: the Marine Regions EEZ v12 "Moroccan Exclusive Economic Zone", with coastal gaps closed (149 km²).
  3. Key Biodiversity Areas. The World Database of KBAs, September 2026: 64 polygons and 3 points with a reported area, which are buffered into circles like WDPA points; 424 trigger-species records. Licensed data; don't republish the polygons.
  4. Management effectiveness. GD-PAME evaluations on the 429 site pages: 11 sites carry at least one.
  5. Ecosystems, mountains and inland waters. RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017, Marine Ecoregions of the World, GMBA v2.0, HydroRIVERS and GLWD v2, clipped by 00_prepare_layers.py.
STEP 02Sheet: Setup

Fill in Setup and the Western Sahara question

The geoBoundaries outline for Morocco includes Western Sahara, a territory on the UN list of non-self-governing territories. Protected Planet, the Marine Regions EEZ and the WDPA all keep it separate: Protected Planet's land area for Morocco is 414,105 km², the "Moroccan EEZ" stops at Western Sahara's waters, and no Moroccan WDPA or OECM record lies south of 27°40′N.

To stay consistent with all three, the land outline is cut at the 27°40′N parallel, the border between Morocco and Western Sahara. That gives 415,235 km², within 0.3% of Protected Planet's figure. Including Western Sahara would raise the land area to 685,174 km² while adding no protected area, and cut the land figure from 33.7% to about 20%. Six KBAs centred south of the line (the Dakhla area, Baie d'Ad Dakhla, the Laâyoune wetland and three offshore sites) are set aside in KBA_Western_Sahara_excluded.gpkg. Whatever choice a country makes, state it in Setup row 9 and use the same base map for every number.

STEP 03Outside Excel: 03_analysis/scripts/01_gis_flatten.py

Flatten PAs and OECMs in GIS

Protected areas are dissolved into one flat layer; OECMs are measured only where no protected area lies. With 338 OECMs this step matters: the hunting reserves overlap the national parks and the SIBEs, in 216 overlapping pairs.

The procedure (metadata steps 1–6)

  1. Filter. 45 records drop out: 42 with status Proposed (the seven new natural parks, among them Jbel Krouz, Plateau Central and Bouhachem; Draa-Warkziz-Lebtana National Park; five marine protected areas including Mogador, Alboran, Massa and Agadir; the Essaouira archipelago nature reserve; and 28 biological reserves: 11,064 km² in all), and 3 UNESCO biosphere reserves (Arganeraie, Oasis du Sud Marocain, Cédraie de l'Atlas).
  2. Clip and project. Each site is split into land and sea parts and measured in Mollweide.
  3. Dissolve PAs. 9,447 km² summed becomes 8,849 km² on land; at sea, 1,055 km² becomes 800 km².
  4. Erase PAs from OECMs, then dissolve. The OECMs sum to 143,892 km² on land. Removing the protected areas inside them, mainly the national parks of Ifrane (1,195 km²), Iriqui (825 km²) and Khénifra (772 km²), each also listed as a hunting reserve, and removing overlaps between hunting reserves and SIBEs, leaves 131,066 km².
  5. Attribute overlaps. Protected areas first, then OECMs, earliest designation first. The 1994 SIBEs therefore keep their area and the 2021 hunting reserves get what is left.
STEP 04Sheet: PA_OECM_Register

Enter the site register

All 429 records go in. Column E separates protected areas from OECMs and column G carries the governance type, which matters here: every hunting reserve is reported as collaborative governance.

STEP 05Sheet: Headline_3.1

Read the headline

Result. PAs and OECMs cover 33.70% of Morocco's land, so the land element of Target 3 is met, by 3.7 points. They cover 0.33% of its waters (918 km² of 281,295 km²); reaching 30% at sea would take another 83,470 km². The five proposed marine protected areas, Mogador, Alboran, Massa, Agadir and Souss-Massa, would add roughly 1,500 km² once designated.
STEP 06

The OECM question

Without the OECMs, Morocco's land coverage would be 2.13%. The 221 permanent hunting reserves, all reported in 2021, provide 123,267 km², or 29.7 points; the 117 SIBEs provide 7,918 km².

What to check before reporting

An OECM must deliver positive, long-term outcomes for biodiversity, whatever its main purpose (CBD decision 14/8). Hunting reserves are managed for game, and their biodiversity outcomes need to be documented. None of the 221 has a management-effectiveness evaluation in GD-PAME. State the evidence for their OECM status in the report, and give the PA and OECM figures separately.

STEP 07Sheet: Ecosystems

Ecosystems and inland waters

The High Atlas juniper steppe (biome T4) reaches 29.97%, a hair under the line, so the tool marks it "No". Two of the four biomes reach 30%: temperate forests (33.4%) and deserts (53.4%).

Finding. The Mediterranean woodlands and forests, Morocco's largest ecoregion (145,990 km²), are 14.9% covered, and the argan woodlands 20.8%. Inland waters are poorly covered: 21.6% of lakes and wetlands, with marshes at 13% and riverine wetlands at 17%. At sea, the Saharan Upwelling ecoregion, 252,000 km² of Morocco's waters, is 0.1% covered.
STEP 08Sheet: KBA_Coverage · script 02_kba_overlay.py

Key Biodiversity Areas

Finding. On average, Moroccan KBAs are 58.5% covered, but only 24.2% by protected areas; the OECMs more than double the figure. Four KBAs have no coverage at all, including the 6,740 km² Canary Current shelf 2 offshore. The largest uncovered KBA areas are the two Canary Current shelf sites (10,649 km² together), Wad Lakhdar (2,911 km²), Msseyed (2,477 km²), Oued Mird and the Eastern Middle Atlas.

The PA-only results match the KBA database's figures for every KBA it reports, which confirms the protected-area layer. Four foreign KBAs overlap Morocco's land or waters: two Algerian sites by 3–4 km² along the border, and two Spanish sites, the Strait of Gibraltar (37 km²) and the Chafarinas Islands (275 km²), whose waters lie inside the Moroccan EEZ as drawn by Marine Regions. They are listed in kba_foreign_overlapping_MAR.csv and not counted.

STEP 09Sheet: Governance

Governance

Finding. Unusually, most of the covered land, 88.1%, is under shared governance: all 221 hunting reserves are reported in the WD-OECM with GOV_TYPE "Collaborative governance". Who shares that governance is not recorded; document it in the report. Protected areas and SIBEs are all government-governed. No site is governed by Indigenous peoples or local communities, and every site reports a governance type, so the QC check passes.
STEP 10Sheet: Mgmt_Effectiveness

Management effectiveness

Eleven site pages list GD-PAME evaluations: METT assessments of Toubkal and Ifrane (2013), Talassemtane (2005) and Sebkha Bou Areg (2014), IMET assessments of Tazekka and Al Hoceima (2018), BirdLife IBA monitoring (2001) and a BREMi evaluation of the Oasis du Sud Marocain biosphere reserve (2013). The Al Hoceima evaluation sits on the SPAMI record and was moved to Al Hoceima National Park, which holds the area; the biosphere-reserve evaluation stays on its excluded record.

No evaluation is less than five years old, so no covered area has a current effectiveness assessment. The ten evaluated sites cover 2.4% of the covered land and 56% of the covered sea.

STEP 11Sheet: Time_Series

Time series

Protected-area coverage has grown slowly since Toubkal National Park in 1942, reaching 2.13% in 2019. The SIBEs added 1.9 points in 1994. Then, in 2021, the 221 hunting reserves added 29.7 points in a single year. At sea, coverage has never exceeded 0.33%.

STEP 13Sheet: Dashboard

Report

In 2026, protected areas covered 8,849 km², or 2.13% of Morocco's terrestrial and inland water area (excluding Western Sahara), and other effective area-based conservation measures a further 131,066 km², or 31.56%, mostly permanent hunting reserves under shared governance; together 33.70% (Headline Indicator 3.1; WDPA/WD-OECM October 2026; Mollweide equal-area). Marine and coastal coverage was 919 km², or 0.33%; reaching 30% would require a further 83,470 km². On average, Morocco's 67 Key Biodiversity Areas are 58.5% covered, 24.2% by protected areas alone. Coverage ranges from 14.9% of Mediterranean woodlands and forests to 67.8% of Mediterranean dry woodlands and steppe. 88% of the covered land is under shared governance; no covered area has a management-effectiveness evaluation from the last five years.
STEP 14

What this example leaves out

  1. Western Sahara. Excluded from land, sea and KBAs to match Protected Planet. A national report that includes it must also change the denominators.
  2. The OECM evidence. The tool counts the hunting reserves and SIBEs as reported; checking them against CBD 14/8 is a national task.
  3. Proposed sites. 42 proposed protected areas, 11,064 km², are excluded until their status changes in the WDPA.
  4. Marine biomes and effectiveness scores. Shelf versus pelagic waters need bathymetry; GD-PAME gives no scores.
